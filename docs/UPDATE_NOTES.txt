[h1]◆ Window 9: Transit Animations ◆[/h1]

[h1]✦ New: colonists actually climb the stairs[/h1]
Crossing between levels used to be an instant hop: a pawn touched the stairwell
and simply appeared on the other side. Every link now plays a real crossing:

[list]
[*][b]Stairs and grand stairs[/b]: taken tread by tread, five distinct steps down
into the well or up out of view, leaning into each step, shrinking away below you
or growing past you above.
[*][b]Ladders[/b]: the pawn pauses at the shaft, leans over to check, then
commits with two big pulls. Coming the other way, the head pops out first before
they haul themselves clear of the hole.
[*][b]Elevator[/b]: a freight lift. The platform takes the weight with a clank
and a puff of dust, drops fast enough to shake, and lands with a bounce. Longer
rides take longer: every level of travel adds to the trip.
[/list]

Both ends play their half. Watch the departure level and they climb away from
you; watch the arrival level and they climb out toward you.

[h1]✦ The honest cost[/h1]
Crossings now take a beat: about half a second on stairs, a little more on a
ladder, and boarding plus travel time on the elevator. Under the hood the
crossing still completes instantly, so pathing, hauling, jobs and orders are
exactly as reliable as before; the pause is only the animation getting time to
read. Prefer the old instant hop? It is one checkbox in settings: Transit
animations.
