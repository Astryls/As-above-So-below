[h1]◆ Window 10: Wall Risers ◆[/h1]

[h1]✦ New: wall risers[/h1]
Utility columns carry a service between levels, but they stand in the middle of
the floor. Risers do the same job from inside the wall.

[list]
[*][b]Four risers[/b], one per service: power, pipe, climate and data. Each joins
the two levels it touches exactly as the matching column does.
[*][b]They mount on a wall[/b], the way a wall lamp does, so the floor stays
clear and a corridor keeps its full width.
[*][b]They do not hold up a roof.[/b] A riser is a fitting, not a support. If you
need the roof held, that is still the column's job.
[*][b]Remove the wall and the riser goes with it[/b], rather than being left
floating in the gap.
[/list]

[h1]✦ Columns and risers no longer need research[/h1]
Both used to sit behind Electricity and Machining. They are how a colony gets
from one level to another, which is the whole point of the mod, so they are
available from the moment you land. The individual network toggles still respect
whatever the owning mod asks for, so an unresearched network still cannot be
carried.

[h1]✦ A tidier build menu[/h1]
The vertical links had been sharing a slot with vanilla's doors and scattered
themselves through the Structure tab. Everything this mod adds is now one
deliberate block after vanilla's own: ladders, stairs, grand stairs and the
elevator, then the four columns, then the four risers.

[h1]✦ Blueprints show what you are actually building[/h1]
Placing one of this mod's buildings drew a plain blue box. Blueprints and the
placing ghost now show the finished building's own art, tinted the usual
blueprint blue, so a planned stairwell reads as a stairwell before a single
block is hauled.

[h1]✦ Fixed: stray plumbing at columns and risers[/h1]
With Dubs Bad Hygiene, Rimefeller or Rimatomics installed, loose lengths of pipe
could appear at a column or riser, on its own level and the one above, looking
like something you had built by mistake. Those fittings are now hidden in the
normal view on every one of those mods' drawing paths. They remain fully visible
in the pipe and power overlays, which is where you go to read what is connected
to what.

[h1]✦ Playing as one character: Perspective Shift[/h1]
Perspective Shift is now supported, and the combination works properly across a
column of levels.

[list]
[*][b]Shooting between levels works while you are the character[/b], through the
same openings and the same rules the colony AI uses.
[*][b]Looking at another level holds the view where you put it[/b] instead of
dragging the camera back toward your character and stranding it between levels.
[*][b]Your character holds still while you look elsewhere[/b], and cannot move or
shoot until you come back. There is a setting in the camera tab to turn that off
if you would rather keep walking around off screen.
[*][b]Taking the stairs cuts straight to the new level[/b] rather than sliding
the camera the whole way there.
[/list]

[h1]✦ A note on the riser art[/h1]
The riser textures in this build are placeholders. They read correctly at a
glance and they are not the final set; proper art is being made and will land in
a later update. Nothing about how they work will change.
