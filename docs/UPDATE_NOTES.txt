Update notes (window 15)

Climate:
- Altitude temperature offsets now ship OFF by default. New colonies get no per-level temperature change unless you opt in.
- Mod settings gained climate presets (Off / Gentle / Standard / Alpine), a master toggle, and a live status line showing whether the temperature patch is active.
- Existing installs keep their configured offsets and existing colonies keep their frozen climate snapshots. Nothing changes mid-save.

Performance:
- The see-below resolver is now cached per map, removing the biggest frame spikes when many cells change at once.
- The temperature patch now unloads itself entirely while no map needs it, so it costs nothing when unused.
- The below-view render and overlay passes are skipped while every level below the view is still undiscovered, which is most of the time for most colonies.

Blueprints:
- Stairs, ladders and elevators now show a proper vanilla-style blueprint (blue outline wash) instead of looking like the finished building.

Perspective Shift:
- The drafted avatar now faces your cursor correctly when the cursor is over open air on another level.
- An avatar carrying something who clicks a stairway, ladder or elevator now travels through it (still carrying) instead of putting the load down on it. Elevators offer the destination menu.
