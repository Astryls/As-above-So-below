[h1]Colonists finish beside the staircase, not on it[/h1]

Order a colonist up or down with the right click menu on a staircase or a ladder and they now finish the trip on the tile in front of the opening, instead of walking back onto the staircase and standing in the middle of it.

The arrival was already right. The order was not: a colonist was set down neatly in front of the stairs on the far level and then immediately walked back on, because the order itself still pointed at the staircase. This is most obvious with Perspective Shift, where a colonist standing on a staircase is drawn inside the art rather than beside it.

[b]Elevators are unchanged.[/b] Colonists still ride to the car and arrive inside it, which is where you want them.
