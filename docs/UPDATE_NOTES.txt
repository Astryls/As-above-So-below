[h1]◆ Window 8: Field Fixes ◆[/h1]

[h1]✦ Fixed: pipe columns spammed errors with Vanilla Expanded pipe mods[/h1]
With any Vanilla Expanded pipe network installed (nutrient paste, helixien gas,
chemfuel and the rest), a pipe column carrying that network printed a red
KeyNotFoundException every time that part of the map redrew, and the conduit that
should appear on the floor above the column never drew at all.

The cause: the VE pipe framework looks its pipe art up in a table built from each
mod's own authored pipe list, and the column's auto-generated connectors are in no
authored list. The connector is now registered with the exact art its source pipe
already uses, so the floor above a pipe column shows the real conduit, it links
into pipe runs laid beside it, and the errors are gone.

[h1]✦ Fixed: Biomes! Caverns basements full of dying plants and bare marsh[/h1]
With Biomes! Caverns, a cavern basement generated most of its fungal trees and all
three marsh mushroom species already dying, while the wet marshy ground stayed
empty.

Our plant seeding never asked the game's own "can this species live on this
terrain" question, so marsh species landed on dry soil and soil species in the
marsh. Seeding now runs the vanilla legality check on every cell: fungal trees
stand on soil, marsh mushrooms stand in the marsh, and open marsh water carries
its hydrophytes at natural density. Mud staying bare matches vanilla swamps and is
correct. Applies to newly generated colonies.

[h1]✦ New: level gizmos got icons, and they select the other end[/h1]
[list]
[*]Stairs, ladders and elevators now show a proper icon for each linked level,
with the arrow telling you whether that end is above or below you.
[*]Using the gizmo selects the matching stairs, ladder or elevator car on the
level you arrive on, so you can walk a whole column by clicking the same spot.
[*]The camera cuts straight to the other end instead of panning across the map.
[/list]

[h1]✦ Verified: Smooth Terrain[/h1]
Smooth Terrain (with NANAME Floors) has been checked against banded colonies:
every level gets its terrain corners smoothed at generation, caverns and sky
plateaus included, and the seams between levels stay clean. No patch needed on
either side. One small pre-existing issue in Smooth Terrain itself (it can quietly
remove swamp and water plants standing on the cells it smooths, on any map, with
or without this mod) has been reported upstream with a suggested fix.
