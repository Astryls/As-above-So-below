[h1]◆ Window 11: Taking The Stairs ◆[/h1]

[h1]✦ Colonists actually climb now[/h1]
Crossing between levels used to be instant, with the colonist appearing at the
far end and a faint copy left behind at the stairs for a moment. That was
backwards, and it is gone.

[list]
[*][b]They walk to the stairwell first[/b], at their own speed, along a real
path, and they step onto the stairs before anything happens.
[*][b]Then they climb.[/b] Going down they sink and shrink into the opening;
going up they rise out of it. Stairs get a slight lean, ladders are climbed
square on, and there is a footfall bob timed to the climb rather than to the
distance.
[*][b]They emerge at the far end[/b] instead of blinking into existence in the
middle of the floor, arriving from above when they came down and from below when
they came up.
[*][b]Their name tag travels with them[/b] and they face the way they are
actually going.
[*]A crossing takes about a second and a half. Ladders are slower, and an
elevator ride is a little longer for every level it passes.
[/list]

[h1]✦ Ways between levels are researched now[/h1]
Four new projects, each one standing on its own with nothing required before it.

[list]
[*][b]Stairs[/b] and [b]Ladders[/b] are neolithic and cheap. A standard colony
already knows both when it lands, so nothing about a normal start changes.
[*][b]A tribal colony starts without them[/b] and has to research one before it
can leave the level it arrived on. This is deliberate. It makes digging down an
early goal rather than a freebie, and both projects are the cheapest in the mod.
[*][b]Transfer columns[/b] covers the four utility columns and their four wall
risers. [b]Elevator[/b] covers the elevator.
[*][b]The grand staircase comes with stairs[/b] rather than needing its own
project. It is a wider version of something you already know how to cut.
[/list]

This reverses part of the last update, which removed research from the columns
entirely. The reason that was done still holds: a new colony must not be locked
out of joining its own levels until mid game. So the new project needs nothing
before it and can be started the moment you land. As before, the individual
network toggles still respect whatever the owning mod asks for, so an
unresearched network still cannot be carried.

[h1]✦ Turn the levels off[/h1]
There is now an [b]Enable multiple levels[/b] checkbox on the map size screen,
next to the level plan.

[list]
[*][b]Unticked, the colony generates as an ordinary single level map[/b] and
RimWorld's own map sizes are available again, including the ones this mod
normally locks.
[*][b]Your level plan is remembered, not thrown away.[/b] Tick it back on and
the levels you had chosen are still there.
[*][b]It is per colony[/b], like the rest of the plan, so one settlement can be a
deep column and the next one flat.
[*]Colonies you have already started are not affected either way.
[/list]

[h1]✦ Toggleable Overlays supported[/h1]
If you use Toggleable Overlays, the two now agree about the level below.

[list]
[*][b]Hovering something on a lower level shows its overlay[/b], the same way
hovering it on your own level does. Stack counts, storage labels, bed and throne
names and fuel warnings on lower levels used to be hidden permanently.
[*][b]Forbidden markers match[/b] between the level you are on and the one below.
[*][b]Its zoom setting applies to both levels[/b], so zooming out no longer
leaves the level below cluttered with labels yours has already dropped.
[*][b]Its blueprint transparency slider now drives ours too.[/b] If your plans
look solid rather than translucent, that slider is set to fully opaque; open the
Toggleable Overlays settings and turn it down.
[/list]
