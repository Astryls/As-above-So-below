Why the elevator is the slow, comfy one

Stairs and ladders animate the pawn; the elevator animates the vehicle. Car and rider share one transform, so the ride is a single scale + fade with a slight running vibration, bookended by a gate close and an arrival ding. Its trade is explicit: the longest latency of the three, but one ride crosses any number of levels (linksAllLevels pairs the shaft member on every level), and the per-level cost is a slider. Boarding has a small weight dip when the pawn steps on - cheap, and it sells the platform as physical.
