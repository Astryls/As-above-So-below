What this proposes

The entry clip (shrink into the well, or climb up and out of frame) plus a short shaft hold, in front of today's already-working pop-out. Entry default 18t mirrors the built-but-unwired OutTicks; emerge default 22t is the live PopTicks; the 0.32 floor is MinScale; the shimmy is the shipped one (amplitude 0.10 cells, rate 17). New grammar: an arrival from above settles down from 1.14 instead of growing from 0.32, so up and down journeys read differently on both ends.

Implementation guardrail (run #297)

The hold is real latency, but it must not sit on the carry decision: ABStairAnim.ReadyToCarry gating TryConsumeArrival is the exact thing that broke cross-level movement. Proposed mechanism instead: the carry stays instant; the arrived pawn is briefly immobilized on the far side (stagger-style, self-clearing) while a visual-only ghost plays the entry clip at the origin mouth, then the pop-out plays. Felt result is identical to this mockup; the pather never waits on a cosmetic (rule 30: a felt duration is a chain, not a number).

Honesty notes

Placeholder geometry only - this is a motion study, not proposed art. In game, every motion here is the pawn draw matrix (scale, offset, rotation via the existing GetDrawParms postfix) plus overlay motes; the stair sprite itself never animates. The alpha fades need a tint/shader path we have not built; if that is expensive, the clips still read correctly scale-only.
