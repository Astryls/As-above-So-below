What makes a ladder read as a ladder

Three things, all cheap: the pawn snaps onto the ladder cell first (the mount), progress then advances in four discrete pulses (a staircase easing curve, not a ramp - each pulse is a quick pull between two dwells), and the body rocks left-right alternately with a slight lean each pull. Stairs glide; ladders tick. Defaults are a touch slower than stairs (climbing is effortful), which also gives the two link types different personalities for free.

Implementation guardrail (run #297)

Identical to the stairs page: the mount + climb + hold latency must not gate the carry (ABStairAnim.ReadyToCarry on TryConsumeArrival broke movement once). Carry instantly, immobilize briefly on the far side, play the climb as a visual-only ghost at the origin mouth, then the emerge clip. The stepped easing and rock live entirely in the pawn draw matrix, same as the stairs shimmy.
