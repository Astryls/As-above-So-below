Waste is a sawtooth

current shipping tier 200, worst case vanilla map size
Waste climbs steadily as a band grows, then collapses the instant it crosses a 64-cell boundary and buys a whole new Slot. Heights just under a boundary are nearly free; heights just over one pay for up to 63 rows of nothing. That is why 190 is efficient and 200 is not.
