How to use.
  1. Connect mod folder… and pick this mod's root — the folder containing About/ and Textures/ (named 50b4332c2547). Grant write permission.
  2. Every texture file has its own complete prompt. Click a slot to show it, hit Copy prompt, paste into Nano Banana with a reference image attached.
  3. Drag the result onto that slot — it is written straight into Textures/Things/Building/Risers/ under the filename RimWorld expects. Drop anywhere on the page to match many files by name at once.
Any square transparent PNG works — 256×256 or larger is ideal, RimWorld scales it down. A 1×1 building renders at roughly 64px on screen, which is why the prompts insist on so little detail.
Rotation — three files, not four. Risers marked rotatable ×3 are wall-mounted cabinets using Graphic_Multi. RimWorld's Graphic_Multi.Init falls back to _east with westFlipped when _west is absent, so you only author _north _east _south — exactly what Dubs Bad Hygiene ships for its own boilers and vents. Mirroring is free and guarantees the two side views can never drift apart.
  • south — facing you, wall behind. Front face and all its detail visible.
  • north — facing away. You see the back: nearly plain, with the cable stubs standing proud above the top edge.
  • east — side on. Narrower, taller silhouette, stubs hooking out of the upper left.
